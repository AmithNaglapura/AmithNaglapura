### 👋 About me

I'm an **AI Automation Engineer at [Stronghold Preservation](https://amithnaglapura.github.io/AmithNaglapura/#projects)** in Belfast, where I'm turning paper-based damp and timber site surveys into a connected digital workflow.

I work where messy real-world data meets a team that needs the answer today. That's taken me through post-merger automation at **TP ICAP**, DCF valuation and equity research at **QSMF**, and an **MSc in Artificial Intelligence in Business (First)** at Queen's University Belfast.

- 🔨 **Building:** the Stronghold Site Survey app, with on-site capture, reviewer sign-off and Outlook + Notion integrations
- 📝 **Researching:** retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for time-series data
- 🎮 **Side project:** *Sky Road*, a Pygame driving game from my studio, Antilope Studio
- 🌍 **Languages:** English, Hindi, Kannada · German (intermediate) · French (beginner)
