    About

    Where finance meets intelligence

    An engineer by training, an analyst by practice, and an automation builder by choice.

    I'm Amith Naglapura Phanindra, an AI Automation Engineer based in Belfast. I grew up in Karnataka, India, and studied Electronics & Communication Engineering at Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering in Mysuru, where I learned to bridge hardware and software.

    I moved to Belfast for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence in Business at Queen's University Belfast, specialising in AI strategy and implementation, and graduated with a First. Along the way I automated post-merger operations at TP ICAP, valued companies with DCF models at QSMF, and won hackathons with AI for the NHS and for fraud detection.

    Today I'm at Stronghold Preservation, turning paper-based site surveys into a connected digital workflow. I like problems where messy real-world data meets a team that needs the answer today.
